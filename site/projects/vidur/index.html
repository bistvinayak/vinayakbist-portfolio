Vidur

A browser assistant that does more than summarize. Vidur finds what is actionable on a page, suggests the next question, and helps you move from reading to doing.

The product idea

Turn browsing into progress.

Most page summarizers compress information. Vidur is designed around the decision that comes after reading: what matters, what should happen next, and what question would make the research sharper.

It combines a browser side panel, URL-based conversation history, site-specific skills, model choice, voice, sharing, and a confirmed action flow that lets a vision model locate an element before a click is executed.

Core capabilities

A research copilot with memory.

🧠

Structured understanding

Summaries, actionable items, red flags, and follow-up questions are generated from the page context.

🧩

Reusable skills

Job postings, products, LinkedIn profiles, PDFs, and search results each get a tailored analysis path.

🎤

Natural interaction

Voice input, read-aloud output, copy, sharing, and per-page conversation threads keep the flow lightweight.

✅

Confirmed actions

When Vidur proposes a click, the user sees the screenshot and target before anything is executed.

Build notes

Designed around trust.

Vidur keeps the user in control of sensitive browser actions, makes the model output inspectable, and supports free-model fallback chains so the product stays accessible during exploration.
